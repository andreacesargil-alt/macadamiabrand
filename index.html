Macadamia · Since 2010

Every person has
their own way of dressing.

Macadamia is an independent fashion brand founded in 2010 by textile designer Andrea César. The project explores fashion through experimentation, craftsmanship and individuality, creating garments that reflect the personality, stories and identity of each person.
